# Quake Anthology 1.1.3

Patch release for the shared TypeScript engine for Quake, Quake II, Quake III,
their expansions, and supported rerelease content. This release focuses on the
custom-game builder, cross-game play in Quake III worlds, and engine
responsiveness.

## Highlights

- The custom-game builder is one page: World, Player, Match, and Kit on a
  single screen. Long lists open as scrollable dropdowns on the same page,
  grouped under Quake, Quake II, and Quake III Arena categories that expand
  and collapse. A Classic/Rerelease toggle selects which edition of the
  chosen title is in effect; the list itself is unified and does not change
  between editions.
- Character selection shows a rotating turntable preview while you choose:
  Q1 shirt and pants colors, Q2 model and skin, and Q3 model and skin are
  all visible as you pick them. Map selection is nested under its map set,
  and appearance options are nested under Character.
- Settings are grouped into submenus with a standardized type scale, and
  input tuning persists globally and immediately (bindings stay per game).
- Startup shows the title splash immediately and loads in seconds instead
  of roughly half a minute.

## Gameplay

- Quake III single player is now the bot match it should be: arena bots
  spawn with foreign characters and arsenals, and the Enemies selection on
  Q3 maps chooses which game's player models the bots use.
- Q1 and Q2 characters, movement, and complete arsenals work in Q3 worlds,
  including view weapons that stay anchored to the camera while strafing
  and turning, and portals that show their destination.
- Interactables (ladders, triggers, buttons, doors) work under every
  movement type in every game world, including QuakeWorld movement.
- The in-game LLM keeps one primed session for the whole program run:
  console context is sent while you are still choosing a game, so answers
  no longer take minutes.
- Quake II installations that ship their own `music/` directory now play
  it; the rerelease soundtrack remains the fallback.

## Fixed

- Fixed a crash when arena bots fought with foreign arsenals (`Bot weapon
  0 has no loaded weapon information`), bots aiming at walls instead of
  the player (spawn delta angles were applied twice to bot aim), and
  `+back` doing nothing under NetQuake movement in Q3 worlds (the spawn
  knockback timer never aged under foreign movement).
- Fixed `boss2` under Q3 movement, the Jorg shell, presentation
  interpolation hitches, and incomplete Q3 turntable models and skins.

## Download and run

The binary download targets Linux x64. Extract `quake-anthology-1.1.3-linux-x64.tar.gz`
next to your game folders (or anywhere under them), open its directory, and run:

```sh
./quake-typescript --menu --renderer gl
```

The executable finds `id1`, `baseq2`, and `baseq3` in its own folder, the
folder above, or Steam libraries. Use `--content-root /path/to/qfiles` to
point at a specific collection, or `--steam` to search Steam first.

The executable includes Bun. Install SDL2, OpenGL, FreeType, libvorbisfile, and
libtheoradec. Supply your own game files; no retail game data is included.
See [setup](../getting-started.md) and [content layout](../content-layout.md).

## Known issue and scope

Native DLL gameplay can still suffer low frame rates and stalls, as in 1.1.2.
That remaining performance work is scheduled for a later release. The HUD is
still the unified HUD rather than each game's native HUD; per-game HUDs are
tracked as future work, and the builder's Classic/Rerelease toggle is the
selection that will drive them.

Two gameplay bugs also carry over from 1.1.2: Quake III `give`/`giveall`
cheats do not grant items, and the Quake II Brain's power screen mishandles
shots from foreign (selected) arsenals. The plan validator in this
repository also expects a sibling `../quake-1-re-ts` checkout and reports
it missing without one.

Saving and loading are accepted for this release. Mod compatibility requires
supported executable interfaces and component declarations; discovering a package
does not prove compatibility. See [mod compatibility](../mod-compatibility.md).

Engine source is GPL-2.0-or-later. The source for this release is tagged `v1.1.3`
in the repository. Game data retains its original licensing.
