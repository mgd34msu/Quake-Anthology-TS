# Quake Anthology 1.2.6

Bug-fix patch: tossed items (most visibly Quake monster backpacks)
could wedge floating in mid-air against walls and corners and never
land, leaving them unreachable.

## Fixed

- **Dropped backpacks and other tossed items no longer hang in
  mid-air.** The shared physics step for tossed bodies made a single
  push attempt per step: on contact with a steep surface or a brush
  corner it clipped the velocity once and stopped. From then on every
  step replayed identically — gravity re-added the into-surface
  velocity component the clip had removed, the movement trace again
  reported no progress from the resting contact, and the item hung
  frozen in place forever (its stored velocity grew without bound:
  observed past 1200 units/s downward while the position never
  changed by a single bit). The step now retries with the clipped
  velocity within the same step, the way FlyMove bumps do, so a
  wedged body slides along the contact and comes to rest on the
  floor where it can be picked up. Trajectories that already moved
  freely are unchanged. Verified headlessly with a real dropped
  backpack from a guest Quake world under a Quake II host: before,
  it froze mid-air at the corner indefinitely; after, it slides
  down, rests grounded on the floor, and walking onto it grants the
  ammo through the cross-game supply mapping.
