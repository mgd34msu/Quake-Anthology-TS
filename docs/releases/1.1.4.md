# Quake Anthology 1.1.4

Patch release for the shared TypeScript engine for Quake, Quake II, Quake III,
their expansions, and supported rerelease content. This release completes the
edition and mode matrix: every campaign plays under every edition of its game,
and the deathmatch and horde modes play in every game, including custom games.

## Highlights

- Classic editions play rerelease campaigns as true classic products, with
  classic engine behavior and the campaign's own program, entities, and HUD:
  Dimension of the Past, Dimension of the Machine, Dawn of the Machine, and
  Quake 64 under classic Quake; Call of the Machine and Quake II 64 under
  classic Quake II. QuakeWorld rows exist for every Quake expansion, and the
  catalog now covers 40 products.
- Horde mode exists in every game: authored monster waves on Quake horde
  maps under any Quake program, campaign monster waves in Quake II, and
  escalating bot waves in Quake III arenas. Custom games can select horde
  under Match rules in any family.
- Rogue's Tag deathmatch mode plays in every game, not only Quake II: the
  token spawns on any deathmatch map, the carrier frags for triple score,
  and killing the carrier scores five and takes the token. Quake III
  Capture the Flag is selectable as match rules and runs its native
  gametype with auto-joined, balanced teams.
- Mode presets in the launcher now offer Horde, Tag, DeathBall, and Capture
  the Flag variants with maps chosen from authored mode entities.

## Gameplay

- Quake II Capture the Flag and Loki's Minions CTF accept either edition;
  Tag and DeathBall run on any Quake II program.
- Launch validation cross-checks every selected role's family, so invalid
  custom combinations fail with a specific message instead of falling back.

## Fixed

- Foreign Quake characters no longer render with black shirt and pants
  colors (for example in mirror reflections); uncolored foreign models use
  their builder colors.
- The Quake lightning gun beam pitched the wrong way when aiming up or
  down; the beam now strikes toward the crosshair at any pitch.
- Quake III bot corpses can be gibbed by foreign weapons: point traces hit
  corpses and the gib damage path runs to completion.

## Download and run

The binary download targets Linux x64. Extract `quake-anthology-1.1.4-linux-x64.tar.gz`
next to your game folders (or anywhere under them), open its directory, and run:

```sh
./quake-typescript --menu --renderer gl
```

The executable finds `id1`, `baseq2`, and `baseq3` in its own folder, the
folder above, or Steam libraries. Use `--content-root /path/to/games` to
point at a specific collection, or `--steam` to search Steam first.

The executable includes Bun. Install SDL2, OpenGL, FreeType, libvorbisfile, and
libtheoradec. Supply your own game files; no retail game data is included.
See [setup](../getting-started.md) and [content layout](../content-layout.md).

## Known issue and scope

Native DLL gameplay can still suffer low frame rates and stalls, as in 1.1.3;
a dedicated performance pass across all games is the next milestone. The HUD
is still the unified HUD rather than each game's native HUD.

DeathBall and Loki's Minions CTF remain Quake II modes (their objectives are
authored into Quake II maps), and Quake Capture the Flag runs through the
Threewave product. QuakeWorld expansion products are catalogued but their
launches are not yet verified against retail QuakeWorld data. Runtime horde
and Tag wave state is not yet part of saved games.

Saving and loading are accepted for this release. Mod compatibility requires
supported executable interfaces and component declarations; discovering a package
does not prove compatibility. See [mod compatibility](../mod-compatibility.md).

Engine source is GPL-2.0-or-later. The source for this release is tagged `v1.1.4`
in the repository. Game data retains its original licensing.
