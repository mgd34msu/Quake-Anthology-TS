### T4-002 — Default `toBeCloseTo` cannot see the slider clamp and allows 45% error on mouse coefficients
- **Verification (VERIFY-L): confirmed. Final severity: Low.** tests/settings/mouse-axis.test.ts:58-59 writes 201 and asserts toBeCloseTo(0.044) at default precision; unclamped 0.04422 passes, so the clamp at src/ui/settings/index.ts:179 is unobserved. Line 34's 0.011 tolerates 45%.
- Severity: Low
- Category: test-gap
- Location: tests/settings/mouse-axis.test.ts:34,35,44,57,58-59; src/ui/settings/index.ts:179
- Confidence: reproduced (scratch test passed `expect(0.022 * 201 / 100).toBeCloseTo(0.044)` and `expect(0.015).toBeCloseTo(0.011)`)
- Evidence: `vertical.write(201); expect(builder.mouse.tuning.pitch).toBeCloseTo(0.044);` exists to prove the clamp in `Math.min(200, Math.max(0, value))`. - Category: test-gap
