Final severity: Critical.** QVM `sscanf` always writes all destinations (`game/bg_lib.c:1299-1319`). `_atof` leaves its pointer past the NUL after a final digit (`game/bg_lib.c:884-911`), so the next read uses stale bytes in the same `buf`. Called at `game/ai_cmd.c:1189` and `game/g_spawn.c:68`. Template confirmed at `match.c:213`, reached by team chat or `tell`. C continues; the port throws. Source review: SRCQ3
- Severity: Critical
- Category: bug
- Location: src/core/game-numeric.ts:29-31, :111-115; src/bots/behavior/q3/ai-command.ts:503; src/content/q3/base/game/spawn.ts:85, :172
- Confidence: reproduced
- Evidence: `byte()` has `if (this.offset > this.text.length) throw new RangeError("Game number scan reads beyond its backing string");`, and `take()` advances past the terminator. `scanGameVector` calls `readFloat` three times unconditionally, under the comment "QVM sscanf("%f %f %f") always writes all three destinations". `botMatchCheckPoint` runs `const scanned = scanGameVector(positionText);` with only a `gameType < GT_TEAM` guard and no addressed check. POSITION comes from player text through the retail `botfiles/match.c` template `"checkpoint "|"waypoint ", NAME, " is at ", POSITION`.
